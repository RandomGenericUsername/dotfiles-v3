Rec: swatch keeps the ◐ = ON / ○ = OFF semantics but is CSS-drawn (15px circle, gradient half-fill), never the U+25D0/U+25CB text glyphs — those render font-dependent (tiny, off-center, blobby), which was the "looks weird" in review. Swatch + Apply share one compact floating pill toolbar (no gap, no float-over-thumbnail clash); tooltip names the file (“High-contrast ON for fjord-night.jpg — click to turn off”). Clicking the swatch toggles high-contrast in place (2026-09-18 review) — it is both indicator and control from the grid; the tooltip + card sublabel disambiguate which wallpaper is affected.
