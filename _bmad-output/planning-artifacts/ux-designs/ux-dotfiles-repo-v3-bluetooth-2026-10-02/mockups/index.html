New icon group — bluetooth

There is no Bluetooth icon group for the status bar at all today. bar_mappings exist only on battery, network, btop, power-menu and settings — Wi-Fi rides inside network's states, so it could not be borrowed. A new group with four variants is required.

state keyconditionvariantart
off!bluetoothPowered()bluetooth-off-defaultrune + slash
idlepowered, nothing connectedbluetooth-defaultbare rune
connectedpowered, ≥1 connectedbluetooth-connected-defaultrune + hollow dot
activethe routed device is connectedbluetooth-active-defaultrune + filled dot

The load-bearing rule: bar state is carried by ART, never by TINT. icon_contrast.py rewrites only group-level COLOR_FOREGROUND of BAR_GROUPS; a variant-level pin exempts a glyph from the guard — which is what system-battery-* wants (it must render unretargeted on opaque glass) and exactly what a bar glyph must not have. So the new group declares color_mappings at group level only, no variant pins, and adds "bluetooth" to BAR_GROUPS. All four states stay guard-managed and legible on any wallpaper.

bold/rounded weight variants are deliberately omitted: the network group carries three weights because Wi-Fi strength needs weight to read at 24px; Bluetooth has four discrete states and none of them is a strength ladder. The settings group already proves a single-default bar group is the house norm.

The hub — decision

No new hub topic. Bluetooth stays purely BlueZ-D-Bus, exactly like Wi-Fi, and contracts/event-contract.{json,xml}, hub.KNOWN_TOPICS, emit_validation.py and event-bus-core.ts are all unchanged. The full both-sides argument, and the exact `bluetooth.state` shape that would be correct if a consumer ever appeared outside the AGS bar process, are in EXPERIENCE.md §The hub question.
