What this version is showing

Theme follows the desktop palette

This browser mock uses a fixed sample palette for layout and interaction only; it does not demonstrate runtime theme or contrast. The GTK panel inherits wallpaper-generated AGS colors.css: surface @color_00, edge @color_04, selection @color_10, primary action @color_06, and slider fill @color_13. Slider track/thumb and text reuse shared AGS tokens. The live microphone signal keeps the established semantic green.

Destination and level are separate

The top selector chooses the preferred output. Its level slider changes that device’s master level, affecting every stream routed there, including explicit app routes to C. Apps routed elsewhere are unaffected.

Follow default versus override

Chrome follows the preferred device and displays “Preferred · C”. Spotify is explicitly routed to A. Changing the preferred device should move Chrome while Spotify stays on A.

Media first, app identity still visible

Artwork, title, and artist describe what is playing. The small badge identifies the app that owns the stream. Missing artwork can fall back to the app icon.

App controls stay together

Each row has its own transport, seek, stream volume, and mute toggle. The volume glyph represents that app’s live stream level and mute state.
