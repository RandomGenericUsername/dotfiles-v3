Settings Panel — visual directions

Composition is fixed: macOS Control Center (Wi-Fi / Bluetooth / Hyprmod tiles, then Display + Sound slider cards). What differs below is the visual register — surface treatment, corners, toggle weight, slider anatomy. All inherit the GUI-unification family and the live wallpaper palette. Each direction shows two states: main and the Wi-Fi subview (in-place swap + back arrow).

Direction A

Apple-faithful Control Center

Big filled icon circles, soft 16px glass, chunky 26px sliders with white thumbs. Reassuring and immediately legible; leans on the macOS muscle-memory you referenced.

Palette finding from this mock: on your current wallpaper palette, foreground-on-@color10 (selection) is 4.10:1 and background-on-@color10 is only 2.67:1 — so selected rows use foreground text, not the family's usual background-on-selection rule. @color13 (accent) is 3.09:1 against foreground, so it is used as a fill/indicator only, never as small text. Toggle-on color is the one thing that can't follow the family's @color06 rule cleanly here (@color06 is a dark plum and vanishes on the dark panel) — each direction shows a different answer; that's a decision I'll ask you about next.
